# French

Resources for learning French, organized by skill.

## Courses

- [TV5Monde Apprendre](https://apprendre.tv5monde.com/) - Free exercises and video lessons from the French broadcaster, sorted by level from A1 to B2.
- [RFI Francais facile](https://francaisfacile.rfi.fr/) - News-based exercises from Radio France Internationale, including the daily Journal en francais facile.

## Listening

- [InnerFrench](https://innerfrench.com/) - Podcast about interesting topics, spoken clearly at natural speed for intermediate learners.
- [News in Slow French](https://www.newsinslowfrench.com/) - Weekly news read at a slower pace, with transcripts and grammar notes.
- [Francais Authentique](https://francaisauthentique.com/) - Podcast about learning French, spoken in clear and natural French.

## Grammar

- [Lawless French](https://www.lawlessfrench.com/) - Grammar lessons, quizzes and listening practice organized by level.
- [Le Conjugueur](https://leconjugueur.lefigaro.fr/) - Conjugation tables for every French verb, from Le Figaro.
