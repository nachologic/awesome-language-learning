# German

Resources for learning German, organized by skill.

## Courses

- [DW Learn German](https://learngerman.dw.com/) - Free courses from A1 to C1 by Germany's international broadcaster, including the Nicos Weg video series.

## Listening

- [Easy German](https://www.easygerman.org/) - Street interviews and a weekly podcast with transcripts and vocabulary help.
- [Slow German](https://slowgerman.com/) - Podcast about German culture and language, spoken slowly with transcripts.

## Grammar

- [Your Daily German](https://yourdailygerman.com/) - In-depth blog that explains German grammar and vocabulary with humor.
- [Lingolia German](https://deutsch.lingolia.com/de/) - Grammar explanations with exercises, organized by level.

## Vocabulary & Dictionaries

- [Leo](https://dict.leo.org/) - German-English dictionary with verb tables and active forums.
- [Duden](https://www.duden.de/) - The authoritative dictionary of the German language.
