# Portuguese

Resources for learning Portuguese, organized by skill.

## Courses

- [Practice Portuguese](https://www.practiceportuguese.com/) - Lessons, short podcast episodes and interactive exercises for European Portuguese.
- [Speaking Brazilian](https://speakingbrazilian.com/) - Video lessons and courses for Brazilian Portuguese, from a Brazilian teacher.

## Listening

- [Portuguese Lab](https://www.portugueselab.com/) - Podcast with dialogues, stories and grammar episodes for European Portuguese learners.

## Guides & Tips

- [Rio & Learn](https://rioandlearn.com/) - Free daily lessons about Brazilian Portuguese, from grammar to slang and culture.
