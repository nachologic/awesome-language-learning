# Korean

Resources for learning Korean, organized by skill.

## Courses

- [Talk To Me In Korean](https://talktomeinkorean.com/) - Lessons and podcasts covering everything from the alphabet to advanced conversation.
- [First Step Korean](https://www.coursera.org/learn/learn-korean) - Free university course from Yonsei covering reading, writing, listening and speaking basics.

## Grammar

- [How to Study Korean](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/) - Structured free lessons from Hangul to advanced grammar, with audio examples.
- [GO! Billy Korean](https://gobillykorean.com/) - Clear grammar lessons from beginner to advanced, with a popular YouTube channel.

## Vocabulary & Dictionaries

- [Naver Dictionary](https://dict.naver.com/) - The standard Korean-English dictionary, with example sentences and native audio.

## Guides & Tips

- [90 Day Korean](https://www.90daykorean.com/) - Guides and articles for learning Korean efficiently, from Hangul to conversation.
