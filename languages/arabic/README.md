# Arabic

Resources for learning Arabic, organized by skill.

## Courses

- [Madinah Arabic](https://www.madinaharabic.com/) - Free course for Modern Standard Arabic, from the alphabet to full sentence structure, with audio.

## Listening

- [Learn Arabic with Maha](https://www.youtube.com/@LearnArabicwithMaha) - Popular YouTube channel teaching Modern Standard Arabic and Palestinian dialect.

## Reading & Writing

- [Arabic Reading Course](https://arabicreadingcourse.com/) - Learn to read the Arabic alphabet through short, progressive drills.

## Guides & Tips

- [Arabic for Nerds](https://arabic-for-nerds.com/) - In-depth articles on Arabic grammar for learners who enjoy the details.
