# Italian

Resources for learning Italian, organized by skill.

## Courses

- [LearnAmo](https://learnamo.com/) - Video courses and exercises from A1 to C2, taught by native teachers from Bari.
- [One World Italiano](https://oneworlditaliano.com/) - Long-running free video courses for all levels, taught by Veronica.

## Listening

- [Italy Made Easy](https://www.italymadeeasy.com/) - Lessons and podcasts taught in Italian, aimed at making the jump from English explanations to real comprehension.
- [News in Slow Italian](https://www.newsinslowitalian.com/) - Weekly news read at a slower pace, with transcripts and grammar notes.

## Grammar

- [Online Italian Club](https://onlineitalianclub.com/) - Free grammar lessons and exercises organized by level, from A1 to C2.

## Vocabulary & Dictionaries

- [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/vocabolario/) - The authoritative dictionary of the Italian language.
