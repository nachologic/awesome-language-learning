# Japanese

Resources for learning Japanese, organized by skill.

## Courses

- [Marugoto](https://www.marugoto-online.jp/) - Free online courses from the Japan Foundation, based on real-life communication.

## Grammar

- [Tae Kim's Guide to Japanese](https://guidetojapanese.org/) - Complete free grammar guide, from the writing system to advanced structures.
- [Bunpro](https://bunpro.jp/) - Spaced-repetition grammar practice, with paths for JLPT levels and popular textbooks.

## Listening

- [Natural Japanese (NIJ)](https://www.youtube.com/@nijapanese) - Comprehensible input videos in slow, clear Japanese, sorted by level.

## Reading

- [NHK News Web Easy](https://www3.nhk.or.jp/news/easy/) - Daily news articles rewritten in simple Japanese, with furigana and audio.

## Vocabulary & Kanji

- [WaniKani](https://www.wanikani.com/) - Spaced-repetition system for learning kanji and vocabulary through mnemonics.

## Vocabulary & Dictionaries

- [Jisho](https://jisho.org/) - Fast Japanese-English dictionary with kanji lookup, example sentences and audio.

## Practice & Immersion

- [Japan Cat](https://japancat.co) - AI conversation partner for speaking Japanese out loud that corrects each sentence, explains grammar in English, Cantonese or Mandarin, and tracks recurring mistakes, with JLPT N5-N1 levels.

## Guides & Tips

- [Tofugu](https://www.tofugu.com/) - In-depth guides on learning Japanese, kanji and Japanese culture.
