# Esperanto

Resources for learning Esperanto, organized by skill.

## Courses

- [lernu.net](https://lernu.net/) - Free multilingual courses, a dictionary and a community forum for learners.
- [Wikibooks: Esperanto](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Esperanto) - Open textbook with lessons, grammar and vocabulary, edited by volunteers.
- [Esperantopedia](https://esperantopedia.org/learn/course/) - Free step-by-step course with exercises, available in 17 languages. Run by the person submitting this entry.

## Grammar

- [PMEG](https://bertilow.com/pmeg/) - Plena Manlibro de Esperanta Gramatiko, a detailed reference grammar written in Esperanto by Bertilo Wennergren.
- [Esperantopedia Grammar](https://esperantopedia.org/grammar/) - Short grammar pages with examples, in 17 languages.

## Vocabulary & Dictionaries

- [Reta Vortaro](https://reta-vortaro.de/revo/) - Online dictionary of Esperanto with definitions in Esperanto and translations into many languages.
- [Esperantopedia Dictionary](https://esperantopedia.org/dictionary/) - Word pages with meanings in many languages, built from open sources such as Reta Vortaro and Wiktionary.
- [The 1,000 most common Esperanto words](https://esperantopedia.org/learn/common-words/) - Frequency list from Tatoeba, with meanings in many languages and free CSV and Anki files.

## Practice & Immersion

- [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org/) - Collection of example sentences with translations, including a large set of Esperanto sentences.
