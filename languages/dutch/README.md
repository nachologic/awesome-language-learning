# Dutch

Resources for learning Dutch, organized by skill.

## Courses

- [Learn Dutch with Bart de Pau](https://www.learndutch.org/) - Free video lessons and online courses, from beginner to advanced.
- [Duolingo Dutch](https://www.duolingo.com/course/nl/en/Learn-Dutch) - Gamified course for building basic vocabulary and grammar.

## Grammar

- [Dutch Grammar](https://www.dutchgrammar.com/) - Clear explanations of Dutch grammar with examples and audio.

## Vocabulary & Dictionaries

- [Van Dale](https://www.vandale.nl/) - The authoritative dictionary of the Dutch language.
