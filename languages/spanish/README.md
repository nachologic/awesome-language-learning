# Spanish

Resources for learning Spanish, organized by skill.

## Listening

- [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.dreamingspanish.com/) - Comprehensible input videos in Spanish, sorted from superbeginner to advanced.
- [Notes in Spanish](https://www.notesinspanish.com/) - Long-running podcast with real conversations for intermediate and advanced learners, with transcripts.
- [News in Slow Spanish](https://www.newsinslowspanish.com/) - Weekly news read at a slower pace, with transcripts and grammar notes.
- [Unlimited Spanish](https://unlimitedspanish.com/) - Podcast lessons that build fluency through mini-stories and point-of-view techniques.

## Grammar

- [StudySpanish](https://studyspanish.com/) - Structured grammar lessons with audio and quizzes.
- [Conjuguemos](https://conjuguemos.com/) - Verb conjugation drills and vocabulary games.

## Vocabulary & Dictionaries

- [SpanishDict](https://www.spanishdict.com/) - Spanish-English dictionary with conjugations, example sentences and pronunciation.
- [Diccionario de la lengua espanola (RAE)](https://dle.rae.es/) - The official dictionary of the Spanish language, from the Real Academia.
